Your data stays in this browser on your device. Back it up regularly under Data › Export.

If you change departure or arrival, the duration is recalculated. You can still adjust it manually afterwards.

Import Excel

Choose one or more Excel files (.xlsx). The header row is detected automatically, including the two-row logbook template with departure/arrival. Before importing you see what was recognised. Entries with the same date and departure time are never duplicated, and new entries are sorted by date automatically.

Duration is read as h:mm, decimal hours (1.5) or minutes (90); dates as Excel dates or DD.MM.YYYY. Rows without a date, such as total rows, are ignored.

Write automatically to your Google Sheet logbook

Every new, changed or deleted flight is transferred to your Google Sheet logbook automatically (new flights go to the next free row of the last page; when a page is full, “Seite 12” etc. is created automatically). This only works when the app runs from its own web address (hosted index.html), not in the Claude preview.

Setup (one time)
  1. Open your logbook sheet (the one with the pages “Seite 1”, “Seite 2” …) and tap “Extensions”, then “Apps Script”.
  2. Delete the existing code, paste the code below, enter your own secret password at TOKEN and save.
  3. Create a second, empty Google Sheet for your documents. Copy its ID (the part of its address between “/d/” and “/edit”) and enter it at DOCS_FILE_ID in the script. Licences and documents are written there, never into the logbook.
  4. “Deploy”, “New deployment”, type “Web app”. Execute as: “Me”. Access: “Anyone”. Deploy and allow the permissions (on the “Not verified” warning tap “Advanced” and “Continue anyway”). Already deployed earlier? Choose “Deploy”, “Manage deployments”, the pencil, version “New version”, then “Deploy”. The address stays the same.
  5. Enter the web app URL shown (ends in /exec) and your password below and tap “Sync now”.

Automatic: After every save, edit or delete the change is sent to the sheet immediately. Without internet it is remembered and sent later. The status is shown at the top of the page.
Sync now: syncs new, changed and deleted flights in both directions. With automatic sync enabled this happens every 15 seconds while the app is open and online.
Replace this device: this device gets exactly the state of the sheet.
Deleted: the row in the sheet stays empty (a gap); the formulas are kept.
